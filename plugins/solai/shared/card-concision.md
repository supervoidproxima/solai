# Card concision

Shared by every card skill. This governs LENGTH only. Structure is the class declaration's
job, and tone is the vault's.

## The rule

Cut ballast, never reasoning. A long justification with no other home stays long. What goes
is the six kinds of filler below, and a cut is made only when one of them can be named.

## The six

1. **Restating a linked card.** The link carries it. Summarise in a clause, not a paragraph.
2. **Restating the source.** Cite it. A quotation is not a summary of a quotation.
3. **Narrating the process.** How the finding was reached belongs in the analysis, not the card.
4. **Hedging.** "It appears that", "arguably", "to some extent". Either it is claimed or it is not.
5. **Pre-emptive counterargument** nobody has made yet. Wait for the objection.
6. **Repeating the frontmatter in prose.** The severity is in the field. Do not say it twice.

## Measure before cutting

`py _system/scripts/measure_cards.py <folder>` reports presence, median and maximum length
per section. Cut against the distribution, not against a feeling about one card.
