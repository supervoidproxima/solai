## Handoff at half context

**At roughly 50% context consumption, stop and offer a handoff.** Do not wait for the
window to run short.

Write `_system/handoff/YYYY-MM-DD-topic.md` containing: where the state lives and in which
file, what is done, the numbered next steps, decisions taken, open questions, and the traps
someone would otherwise walk into. Then give a paste-ready block for the next window.

Offer it once. If declined, carry on and do not ask again that session.

Half is the bar rather than three-quarters because a handoff written under pressure is
written from a context already too full to summarise itself accurately, which is exactly
when the summary matters most.
