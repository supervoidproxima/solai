## Operating constraints

- **Zero hallucination.** Missing data is written as `Unknown`. Do not infer a clause, a
  date, a number, or a step. A claim without a source is an assumption, and assumptions do
  not belong in an artefact anyone will act on.
- **Cite at the claim.** A specific number, a named person, a threshold, a quoted phrase or
  anything that drives a recommendation carries its source inline.
- **Read the status before using a card.** Never judge a card's state by the file existing,
  by the body being full, or by something else linking to it.
- **No em-dashes.** Commas, colons, parentheses or a sentence break. Hyphens in compound
  words are fine.
- **No filler.** No preamble restating the request, no closing summary unless asked.
