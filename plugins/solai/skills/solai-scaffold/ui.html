Choose a type.

It sets the folders, the cards and the agents you get.

Give it a name.2

Short is better. It becomes the folder name.

Say what it is for.3

One line, in your words. It goes into CLAUDE.md, so the next session reads it before anything else.

Name what it owes first.4

The first thing this place hands somebody. It is the denominator of every ratio this vault reports about itself later, and naming it is one of the two things that lift the tier.

Choose the languages.5

What you will write cards and notes in. A card is written in one of them, whole: the language may change between cards and never inside one.

Choose where it lives.6

Choose the folder it goes in, or type the path. See what is already inside.

Add your materials.7

Documents prepared for this vault. They are copied in, not read.

Pick a tier.8

How much machinery this place carries. What you may pick is what the folder check and the first artefact support; a tier you cannot hold is shown and refused, never hidden.

Review, then create.9

See every file before it is written. Nothing happens until you say so.
